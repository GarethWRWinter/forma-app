# FORMA Launch Plan

Built on Priestley's Science of Growing Customers (11 Aug 2026). The governing idea:
**only oversubscribed businesses make real profit.** We do not open doors and hope.
We build a visible queue of signals first, then release one hundred places into
proven demand. The banana slide: one hundred places, several hundred people who
want one.

The kill criteria in cycling-coach.md remain the referee. This plan is how we hit
them, not a replacement for them.

## The annual big message (Action 15)

One idea, repeated everywhere, all year:

> **Talent was never the missing ingredient. The system was.**

Every post, letter, and page is a variation on this. Four generations of Winters
had the engine; Team Sky had the system; Forma puts the system in reach. The landing
page hero, the founder story, and the letters all already carry it. Do not invent a
second message this year. Repetition is the strategy (11-7-4: people need roughly
eleven interactions, seven hours, four locations before they buy).

## Ideal customer profile (grounded in channel data, 11 Aug 2026)

Instagram audience insights (the primary traffic source): 80.6% men / 19.4% women.
Age: 35-44 is 31.7%, 45-54 is 25.9%, 55-64 is 16.5%, 25-34 is 17.5%, 18-24 is 2.5%.
Geography: UK 30.4%, US 11.6%, Türkiye 4.9%, Australia 4.4%, Spain 3.1%.

**The ICP: the constrained rider.** 30 to 55, career and often family, 6 to 10
fought-for hours a week, owns a power meter, rides sportives/fondos/club events,
plateaued for a season or more. The boundary is constraint, not age: a 26-year-old
junior doctor qualifies; an early retiree with 18 free hours does not. 74% of the
actual audience is 35 to 64; the unconstrained under-25 is 2.5% of the funnel and
is deliberately not addressed by the copy. Marketing stays sharp on the constrained
rider; the product excludes nobody (nothing breaks at 20 h/week).

Implications:
- UK-first launch is correct: GBP pricing, British English, ICO/GDPR posture.
- US is the clear second market (11.6%); revisit USD pricing after the hundred.
- One in five of the audience is a woman; copy stays "the rider", never male-coded,
  and the founding cohort should surface at least one woman's story early.
- Broadening to the unconstrained rider is a Q1 2027 list-price decision, only
  after the founding hundred prove month-2 retention. Not before.

## Phase 0 · Before any doors open (now)

**Goal: 200+ signals before invite one.** (Principle 2: first make your market.)
A signal = a waitlist email. Secondary signals = replies to Letter 0's question,
Instagram follows, DMs.

Founder actions, one week:
1. Publish the rebuilt landing page (done with this plan).
2. Turn on the letters: Letter 0 automated on join, Letters 1 to 4 weekly. The
   waitlist page now promises this, so it is a commitment, not an idea.
3. Announce the Founding Hundred on Instagram + LinkedIn + the blog: founder-story
   post (the spine), close with the page link. Three locations plus email = the
   four locations of 11-7-4.

**The scarcity is real and stated:** one hundred places because Gareth reads every
founding rider's first month personally. Never manufacture a deadline beyond this.

## The perfect repeatable week (Action 13, the engine)

This is the baseline revenue engine and the founder's non-negotiable calendar,
~6 hours/week, protected in the diary like training:

| Day | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Write this week's letter (bank of 4 exists; keep one week ahead) | 60 min |
| Tue | One founder story or build-in-public post (IG + LinkedIn crosspost) | 45 min |
| Thu | One value post: a marginal gain, a real number, a screenshot of Forma doing something startling | 45 min |
| Fri | Reply to every waitlist reply and DM; personally invite the next batch when open (Principle 10: you own the next step) | 60 min |
| Sat | Ride. Content is downstream of riding; capture one photo or number | 0 min extra |
| Sun | 30-min review: signals count, replies, what post worked; adjust next week | 30 min |

Nine value posts for every direct ask. The sell is the byline.

## Phase 1 · The first ten (doors crack open)

Trigger: 100+ signals on the list, Stripe live, legal pages up.

1. Flip `REQUIRE_INVITE=true`. Mint 10 codes, note "cohort-1".
2. Invite the ten **by personal email, one at a time** (not a blast). Choose repliers
   first: they gave the strongest signal. Buying environment (Principle 5): the
   invite email is personal, names their goal from their reply, and includes their
   code. Founding price honoured from rider one.
3. Announce publicly that the first ten places went out (Principle 8: show the
   queue, not the neediness). "Ten of the hundred are riding. Ninety remain."
4. Watch like a hawk for two weeks: onboarding drop-off by device brand, first-week
   activity, every support email answered same-day by Gareth.

## Phase 2 · Twenty-five, then the hundred

- Cohort 2 (15 more) when cohort 1 shows two weeks of healthy activity. Same
  personal-invite ritual. Public count update each batch: 25/100... 60/100. The
  climbing number is the buying environment.
- Cohort 3 releases to 100 in batches of 15 to 25, gated by the kill criteria
  (month-2 retention ≥ 60%, waitlist adding ≥ 20/week with content running).
- Every founding rider's win is content fuel with permission: a PB, an FTP bump,
  a briefing screenshot. Riders like the reader (real social proof, Principle 3),
  celebrated in the letters and the feed (Action 16: celebrate).

## Quarterly spotlights (Action 14)

- **Q3 2026 spotlight: the Founding Hundred launch itself.** The public counting
  of the hundred IS the event.
- **Q4 2026 spotlight: Mallorca 312 / Sa Calobra, 17 October.** The founder races
  his own goal event, coached by his own product, in public. Briefing screenshots
  on the morning, the wind-coloured route, the debrief after. Win or blow up, it's
  the most honest ad the product can have. Plan the content week in advance.
- **Q1 2027 spotlight: the Founding Hundred results.** Aggregate real numbers
  (FTP deltas, consistency) into one piece: "What a hundred riders did in ninety
  days." This is also the moment to decide list-price opening to the waitlist
  beyond the hundred.

## What we never do

- Discount. (Founding price is an earned position, not a discount.)
- Manufacture scarcity or deadlines beyond the true ones.
- Blast the waitlist. Letters give; launch emails are the only asks.
- Let the repeatable week slip more than one week. The engine is the business.
