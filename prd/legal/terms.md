## 2. Health, safety and the AI coach

This is the most important part of these terms. Please read it before you
ride.

**Forma is an AI coach.** Your plan and every change to it, your ride
debriefs, briefings, the coach's replies in chat and the coach's emails are
written by AI. Race Radio, the voice in your indoor sessions, uses
pre-written cues that are picked automatically from your ride data. We build
Forma carefully and the coach follows safety rules, but no person checks each
message, plan or power target before you see it. AI can be wrong about
training, about your data and about you, and it can sound sure of itself
when it is wrong.

**Forma is not medical advice.** Forma gives general training guidance to
adults. It is not a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian, midwife or
psychologist, and it doesn't replace one. It does not diagnose, treat or
rehabilitate any injury, illness or condition, and it is not a medical
device. When the coach talks about health, food, weight or how you're
feeling, that's general information, and it will point you to the right
professional. Never put off getting medical help because of something Forma
said.

**Nobody watches you ride.** Forma doesn't supervise your sessions and isn't
monitored in real time. It is not an emergency service. If you or anyone
else needs urgent help, call 999 (112 in the EU, 911 in the US). If you're
struggling and need to talk to someone, Samaritans are free, day or night,
on 116 123. In the US, call or text 988.

**See a doctor first if this is you.** We recommend that everyone talks to
their GP before starting structured training or stepping up how hard they
train, especially if you're over 35 or coming back from a long break. You
must get medical advice before your first session, and follow it, if:

- you have a heart condition or high blood pressure, or you've had chest
  pain, fainting or blackouts;
- a parent, brother or sister died suddenly, or was diagnosed with an
  inherited heart condition, before the age of 50;
- you have diabetes, asthma, epilepsy or another long-term condition, or
  you take prescribed medicine regularly;
- you have an injury, or you've had surgery, a concussion or a serious
  illness in the past three months;
- you're pregnant, or you've had a baby in the past 12 months;
- a doctor has told you to avoid hard exercise or to exercise only under
  supervision.

**The health questions.** When you join, Forma asks a few health questions.
Please answer them honestly. A yes doesn't stop you joining. It means we
keep your plan to easy riding, with no hard intervals and no tests, until
you tell us a doctor (or your midwife or physiotherapist) has cleared you.
If your health changes, tell the coach or update your answers in Settings.
We can't check what you tell us, so the coaching can only be as good as the
information you give it.

**Stop riding straight away if you feel:**

- pain, pressure or tightness in your chest, or pain spreading to your arm,
  neck, jaw or back;
- faint, dizzy or light-headed;
- more out of breath than the effort explains;
- your heart racing, fluttering or beating irregularly;
- a sudden, severe headache, confusion or a change in your vision;
- sharp pain, or pain that gets worse as you ride.

Call 999 (112 in the EU, 911 in the US) if chest pain lasts more than a few
minutes or comes back, if anyone collapses, or if symptoms are severe.
Otherwise, get medical advice the same day from NHS 111 or your GP. Don't
ride again until a doctor has said you can.

**Your part.** A plan is a recommendation. You decide whether to ride each
session and how hard to go. You agree to:

- give us accurate information about your age, health, medication,
  injuries, FTP (roughly the most power you can hold for an hour) and
  weight, and keep it up to date;
- tell the coach when you're ill, injured, pregnant, starting new
  medication or coming back from a break;
- ease off or stop whenever something feels wrong, whatever the plan, the
  screen or Race Radio says;
- ride within the law and the Highway Code (or the rules where you ride),
  and choose routes, times and conditions you can ride safely in;
- keep your bike, trainer and kit in safe working order, and set up and use
  your trainer as its maker says;
- never let Forma's screen or audio take your attention off the road.

**ERG mode.** In ERG mode, Forma tells your smart trainer what power to
hold. You stay in control: you can ease off, pause, stop or turn ERG off at
any time. Power targets, audio cues and Bluetooth connections can lag, drop
out or be wrong. If the resistance ever feels wrong, stop pedalling and
press Stop.

**The risks of riding.** Cycling and hard training carry risks that no plan
can remove: crashes, traffic, road surfaces, weather, mechanical failures,
overuse injuries and, rarely, heart problems during very hard efforts.
These risks are part of the sport and Forma can't control them, so weigh
them up each time you ride. What the coach says about weather, routes, kit
and fuelling is general guidance. Check the conditions yourself before you
go.

**Tell us.** If the coach ever says something that seems wrong or unsafe,
email gareth@ridewithforma.com. We read safety reports and use them to make
the coach better.
