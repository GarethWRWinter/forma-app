# Insurance: brief and questions for the broker

For Gareth's broker call, 8 October 2026. Not advice; it sets out what to ask
for and what the insurer must be told. Lines marked CONFIRM need a fact only
you can supply.

## What I need

The core policy is **professional indemnity that expressly covers bodily
injury caused by AI-generated training advice delivered through software,
whether or not the software malfunctioned**. Neither standard product fits on
its own:

- **Fitness PI** assumes a qualified individual seeing clients in person.
- **Tech PI** (Hiscox's, for example) excludes bodily injury unless the
  technology failed to perform its intended function. A plan that worked as
  designed but was too hard for a rider would probably fall outside it.

So I need a broker who can arrange blended tech E&O with bodily injury. Also:

- **Public and products liability**, asking whether products cover extends to
  software under the EU Product Liability Directive from 9 December 2026.
- **Cyber**, because Forma holds health data. Cyber Essentials comes with
  £25,000 of cyber cover.

D&O is low priority. Employers' liability isn't needed until the first hire.

## Questions

1. "Our AI writes cycling training and sets live smart-trainer power. Will PI
   cover bodily injury from that advice, whether or not the software
   malfunctioned?"
2. "Is there any AI, algorithm or automated-decision exclusion or sub-limit?
   Please send the wording."
3. "Is there a medical-advice or medical-malpractice exclusion that could
   catch health conversations with the coach?"
4. "Are claims arising from self-harm or a mental-health crisis raised in an
   AI chat covered?"
5. "Does cover depend on a named qualified person signing off the training
   rules? Which qualification? Does a clinician need to review our red-flag
   scripts?"
6. "Which jurisdictions and territories are covered? Can EU riders be
   included? What would the USA and Canada cost?"
7. "Can the retroactive date go back to our first beta user, and what run-off
   cover is available?"
8. "Which screening, record-keeping, retention and terms requirements are
   conditions of cover?"
9. "Does products liability extend to software under the EU directive from
   9 December 2026?"
10. "Does cyber cover a health-data breach, notification costs and ICO
    costs?"
11. "Does using a third-party AI model provider affect cover?"
12. "What limit and excess do you recommend, and are defence costs inside or
    outside the limit?"
13. "Can this be bound before we launch next week?"
